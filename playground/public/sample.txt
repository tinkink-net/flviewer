Plain text view (sub-kind: plain)
=================================

Raw text, monospace, native scrolling and native selection.
No highlighting, no PanZoom — the stage just scrolls.

Truncation: text beyond 2 MB / 10,000 lines renders only the kept prefix
with an in-view notice and a download hint; a `truncated` event fires.
